# Mikey · openwork basketball ornament

The red paths in ornament.svg are the cut contours for a 76 × 82 mm ornament. There are **22 closed cuts**: 20 artwork openings, one hanging hole and one outside perimeter. The outside perimeter is last. There are no engravings or live font dependencies. The black silhouette represents retained material; the wood-tone image is only a preview.

The two name rails and open construction follow the user's wooden ornaments; the basketball sweeps and this version of the script were individually drawn against the selected Sunburst basketball reference. The large M has an open flourish at the left. The round i dot is joined to the upright rather than hanging as a disconnected disk. Both k loops, the e counter and the long lower y loop are cut openings. A short foot weld between the k and e keeps the bottom of the name supported. The y joins the lower-right ball seam and rail. The lower counter was pulled in and the facing k/e cuts were redrawn during review to widen the wood.

The 21 inner contours are disjoint and lie wholly inside the perimeter. They leave one connected piece. The smallest nominal distance between separate cuts is **1.505 mm**, at the upper k loop. The y loop's lower wall is 1.79 mm. The eyelet has a 3.67 mm opening and at least 2.32 mm of wood to the outside edge. The two horizontal rails measure 2.26 and 2.15 mm; the upper and lower vertical basketball webs have minimum widths of 2.78 and 2.91 mm. Eroding the retained polygon from every cut by 0.75 mm still leaves one connected piece; at 0.80 mm it divides. This is a geometric check, not a prediction of grain strength or kerf. No physical cut or xTool import has been verified.
