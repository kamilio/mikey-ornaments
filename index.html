For the laser, import the Cut SVG and assign the vector to Cut. Black is the material that remains; cut the interior openings before the outer rim. The editable SVG contains the actual Pacifico Regular glyph outlines, so you do not need to install the font. The “k” meets the upper rail and the “y” meets the lower rail. Each ornament is about 90 mm wide. The paths were checked digitally; the designs have not been test-cut.
